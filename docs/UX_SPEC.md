# Interface and camera design

## Visual direction

Use dark charcoal, aged brass and restrained Nordic ornament. Text and item silhouettes take precedence over ornament. The approved concept direction has the minimap at upper right; the old horizontal Skyrim compass is absent when the minimap is active.

The three concept scenes were outdoor exploration, Nordic dungeon combat and an open grid inventory. They are visual references, not exact rendering or feature requirements. Multiple companion portraits and enemy minimap dots in a concept do not prove support for every follower or marker type.

## Gameplay layout

| Area | Intended content | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper left | Player and follower portrait/status strip. | Player plus one supported recruited vanilla humanoid follower: hide an empty slot; show a neutral original silhouette, plus name and health when data is fresh; show "unavailable" or "untested" when data or the configuration is unsupported. |
| Upper right | Compact local minimap and quest text. | Reserve separate bounds; allow scale/toggle; map uses the selected provider's real content. |
| Bottom center | Player resources and up to eight action slots. | Clear selected/equipped state and native counts; keyboard/controller prompts reflect actual bindings. |
| World center | Combat, aiming and interaction feedback. | Keep unobstructed; normal close aiming can take precedence over elevated framing. |
| Bottom left | Existing notifications where feasible. | A new comprehensive combat/event-log system is deferred. |

Do not duplicate both player resource presentations unnecessarily. Portrait/resource grouping may be adjusted after live readability checks. If the chosen minimap permits circular framing, prefer it; a working provider-supported shape beats a fragile custom rendering fork.

## Inventory layout

DYL-164 candidate: at the 1280×720 movie baseline, equipment occupies the left 264-unit panel, the native SkyUI category/search/sort controls and three-column grid occupy the middle 542 units, and the native item card occupies the right 408 units. DYL-168 fits that design into `Stage.visibleRect` with a 16-unit outer margin and proportional fitting; larger stages center it. Physical 720p/1080p, scaled HUD and real GFx readability still require owner captures. The existing bottom bar retains native gold, carry weight/capacity and device-specific action prompts. The non-modal item card stays below SkyUI’s top 55% preview-interaction region and above the measured bottom bar, with 8-unit gaps and uniform fit; the modal card remains centered. Search and gear occupy the category-label row, above the measured native column header, using runtime bounds and a minimum 48-unit search width.

The grid uses eighteen native clips (three columns × six rows). Scrolling advances a row-aligned window over the full filtered/sorted native enumeration, including a partial last row. It has no item-capacity limit; a 500-entry actual-AS fixture reaches the last entry with eighteen clips. Each cell preserves its native backing index and listeners, with the native type/custom icon when loaded, an original broad-type vector fallback, a bounded two-line name, count badge and E/F/S/Q letters for equipped/favorite/stolen/quest. A question mark means the field is unavailable, not false. Existing SkyUI entry fields take precedence; missing flags can use a same-view, same-index/count, unambiguous DYL-163 observation, discarded on list/view invalidation. Full names, damage/armor, weight, value and effects remain in the native item card; no stats are invented.

The equipment panel is read-only, with an original static vector silhouette and slots for head (including hair-only hoods/circlets), body, hands, feet, left hand/shield, right hand, amulet and ring. It displays freshly copied native names and broad-type symbols, Empty for a known empty slot and Unknown for unavailable/conflicting data. Existing worn custom names are copied when present; otherwise the base form name is used. A multi-slot armor appears in each covered slot; conflicting records display Unknown. Native hand objects may include spells and torches. A coalesced 100 ms one-shot refresh follows inventory/view/player-info changes; it does not poll every frame or predict equipment. Close/unload cancels pending presentation work. Inspect and select equipment through its item in the grid; the silhouette slots are display-only, have no focus stop and do not dispatch actions.

Required operations remain native inspect, equip/unequip, consume/use/read, favorite and drop with native quantity/quest restrictions. DYL-163's existing activation/drop gates and quantity continuation are preserved; the favorite toggle and unsupported-thread native fallback retain their owner rulings. Quantity/confirmation dialogs keep native focus/input and appear centrally over an opaque backdrop, with the native item card fitted above it. The equipment, grid and custom status panels are hidden for the duration, then restored on native modal close. No grid action buttons or additional dispatch path are introduced.

Container, barter, pickpocketing, follower trade, crafting, magic, journal and map menus remain native/SkyUI and accessible through the existing exits and Magic switch. Normal native paused inventory semantics remain. This is a source/SWF candidate, not runtime or owner acceptance; see [grid evidence](evidence/p3/grid-inventory.md).

## Camera feel

Aim for a held elevated pitch and moderate perspective compression, with configurable distance. Keep character movement intuitive relative to the camera using the selected existing movement route. Allow yaw/rotation, recenter and zoom. Tight rooms lower/shorten the camera; bow/spell precision can enter a closer view. Emergency return is always available.

The overhead view is an aesthetic and navigation mode, not a promise to keep every ceiling invisible. No per-cell roof editing is required for MVP.

## Input and readability

DYL-168 candidate supports keyboard/mouse and controller through the native player menu. Grid cells use a thicker outline plus corner brackets; the category/grid region has corner brackets to show which region owns navigation. Up from the first row enters categories; Left/Right changes category; Down/Confirm returns without activation. Empty lists still allow categories and native Back. Native sort, quantity, confirmation and keyboard-search controls keep their own focus indicators, caret and cancellation; their rendered visibility requires the owner route.

Pinned SkyUI exposes search only on the PC binding. The candidate adds R3 (right-stick click) in the player grid to open a controller character pad. D-pad moves its outlined `>` selection, A adds a character or chooses Space/Erase/Clear/Done, and B cancels the edit and restores the previous filter. Done applies a native name filter and returns to categories, even for zero matches. The caption beneath the grid records the filter. The pad accepts A–Z, 0–9 and space up to 32 characters; this is sufficient for the English MVP substring search, with no localization rollout. It blocks list actions and consumes input before native search/sort; no gameplay binding or engine hook is added. Keyboard search remains Space plus native text entry. See [Input bindings and focus](CONTROLS.md#input-bindings-and-focus).

Equipped/favorite/stolen/quest use E/F/S/Q and explicit unknown markers; hotbar equipped state adds E. Follower availability/health use words and percentages, with absent/dead slots hidden. Grid and equipment names retain the bounded two-line/32-character ellipsis policy; follower names use 22 characters including ellipsis and width fitting; hotbar names use 12 including ellipsis, and the voice name 60, with width fitting. Full item inspection stays in the native item card.

Required owner captures are 1280×720 and 1920×1080 at 100%, plus 115% HUD scale. A basic ultrawide geometry check uses a nonzero visible-rectangle origin. Automated bounds are design-coordinate checks, not rendered font/readability acceptance; GFx may keep a 1280×720 authored stage on a 1080p display. Record both actual display size and `Stage.visibleRect`.

Pause behavior is explicit: normal inventory pause is allowed; action combat stays continuous while menus are closed. Do not show initiative queues, turn buttons, tactical party orders, artificial ability cooldowns or draggable command paths.

## Presentational scope

Exact concept portraits, live character render, textured parchment map, comprehensive event log and multiple arbitrary companion frameworks are polish/extensions. The MVP must still look like a coherent CRPG interface and deliver the functional grid/minimap/hotbar requirements. A mismatch in artwork is not permission to skip a core function.

## Core HUD candidate — DYL-160

The player panel anchors at bottom center with three engine-fed meters labeled HEALTH, MAGICKA and STAMINA. A read-only native snapshot supplies current values and effective maxima, including temporary modifiers, through the UI's Scaleform bridge. Resource reads run on Skyrim's task queue; Scaleform receives numeric copies at most ten times per second, with loading invalidation and a 500ms freshness limit. Displayed percentages are current/effective maximum; native HUD penalties are not applied a second time. It suppresses duplicate native resource groups only while fresh complete data exists and the current native HUD mode permits the custom panel. Missing/stale data, a missing bridge, disable or reinitialization restores native meters for the current mode. The first runtime candidate had invisible resources. Its callback gate was an unreliable source-level initialization contract; later diagnostics confirmed that using Stage coordinates directly under a translated HUDMenu parent placed the meters off-screen. Panel anchors and scale now convert Stage coordinates through the full parent transform. The initial candidate lacked traces to isolate its cause.

The native crosshair, target/activation prompt, compass and map provider retain their existing authority and placement. The upper-left party panel shows one observed follower (DYL-165) or “Follower data unavailable”; it supplies no fabricated follower status. The existing upper-right map area remains reserved for its provider. No hotbar or Camera dependency is introduced.

Preferences belong to `SkyCRPG_UI.ini`: version 1, startup scale 85–115%, horizontal offset ±120 and vertical offset ±60 movie-coordinate units, with viewport bounds. DYL-168 adds `[HUD] bResetLayout=1` as a startup reset command: restart to use scale 100 and offsets 0/0 while preserving HUD enable and minimap policy. No file is rewritten; keep 1 to retain defaults or set 0 and restart to use the stored scale/offset keys again. It requires the DYL-168 `plugin.cpp` integration patch. This is a bounded startup preference, with no live layout editor. See [core HUD evidence](evidence/p2/core-hud.md) for source/build checks and the separately open gameplay acceptance.

The owner-approved bounded compass bridge hides the native compass only for the exact provider stack and a visible, in-bounds map in normal gameplay after a 500 ms settle. The settle is not proof of a fresh render; the owner accepted stale-frame risk. Missing, hidden or uncertain map state releases the compass. Set `bReplaceCompass=0` to retain it. DEM K toggles the map and L toggles zoom; these are distinct from F6/F8 and hotbar 1–8. The Survival temperature indicator is inside the hidden compass and is also hidden. The DYL-168 bottom stack anchors from `Stage.visibleRect`: hotbar/voice 536×77, a 12-unit gap, then status 420×52, scaled together. Left/right/top margins are at least 16 units, status bottom margin 12. Stage positions/scales are converted through the full HUD parent transform. Follower placement also uses the visible rectangle and 16-unit bounds; ultrawide extends the visible area without moving the center stack to an edge. Native compass/quest anchors and DEM placement retain their own owners. The historical 1080p minimap/active-effect overlap is unresolved: DEM cannot be moved by this module. Exact intersection/edge measurements and owner captures remain in the [DYL-168 route](evidence/p3/input-and-layout.md); no safe-area PASS is inferred for these external elements.

For inventory layout (DYL-168 round 5), every child of the list panel (tiles, category bar and its rule, header and its rule, search row, scrollbar, hint, controller search pad) sits inside the native backdrop's frame lines with at least 8 units of padding (frame lines are 3.05..5.1 units inside the backdrop; content area = backdrop inset by 13.1); the backdrop widens to hold three 165-unit tiles on 172 spacing plus the scrollbar and extends below the six-row grid. The panel's real visible right edge stays at least 16 units left of `visibleRect.x + 0.66 * width`; the equipment/grid block moves left before it is uniformly scaled (at least 120 rendered units per tile in the four layout fixtures). The card column runs from that edge + 16 to the screen edge − 16; the non-modal card is centred in it (same centre for every item), below the top 55% and above the bottom bar, capped at 408 scaled units, with the full status line centred directly above it and hidden whenever the card is hidden, the category bar has focus, nothing is selected or the list is empty. The engine 3D preview is centred horizontally over the card (the menu reports the card column to the plugin; fallback 5/6 W) and scaled so the widest observed model fits the column with 10% spare; vertical placement is the game's. The plugin does this by adjusting `Inventory3DManager`'s in-memory placement for the open player inventory only, never while zooming, and never writes an INI setting; `[Inventory] bManageItemPreview=0` turns it off. Grid-region corners follow the painted tile cells (never clip bounds); the category-region corners span the padded content width; individual tile selection corners are unchanged. On gamepad the native bottom bar shows R3 for search in the player grid. The modal quantity/confirmation layout is unchanged.
