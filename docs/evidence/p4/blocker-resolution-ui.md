## Design and limitations

The inventory gate publishes no action of its own; missing/partial/disabled bridge permits the native action path. The grid's activation and its layout depend on the same gate. No Camera state or service is referenced. The HUD movie is byte-identical to the approved candidate. The fallback remains a source/mock/compiled-movie result until exercised in GFx. The DLL cannot guarantee a log when it is absent or the loader rejects it before entry. Stock/incompatible movie detection remains an external preflight limitation. The INI is read once at startup; no live reload is claimed. Weight logs are bounded and require successful selection sampling; off-thread or unavailable snapshots produce no value. Full two-handed semantics depend on engine hand results and need runtime observation.
