## The rule (`iExteriorSeeThrough` 1 or 2)

Every 0.1 s, in eligible frames, in an exterior cell (`scanExterior`, main thread, from `endFrame`):

1. **Cells.** From `TES::gridCells`, only cells whose 4096-unit square comes within 1536 units of the ground-plane box around the camera, the feet and the 128-unit ring (`cellsAround`; at most 3×3, at the wave 1 framing 2×2). The grid is trusted only if it holds the player's parent cell at the slot the engine's own arithmetic gives for the feet (`exteriorCells`); otherwise that scan walks nothing (`gridSkips`), and after 10 such scans in a row FCS-63 is logged once. Cells not attached (`cellState != 7`) are skipped.
2. **Picks** (`castExteriorRays`, one world read lock, `L_LOS` with the player's group, as wave 1; the pick slot audited per vtable, `pickAudited`), in this order: (a) the five wave 1 **floor probes**, each marched past 2 hits; if any of them fails, the scan stops and claims and hides nothing (`floorFailed`); (b) one **upward pick** from 160 over the feet to the camera height, marched past up to 4 hits (`overhead`); (c) **sightline rays** from the rendered camera to 19 targets: head +120, chest +80, hip +40, eight points 64 units out at +80, eight points 128 units out at +24 (`exteriorTargets`), each marched past up to 4 hits, keeping each distinct reference's highest hit point (at most 48 references); (d) for at most 12 of them whose highest hit could be an overhang, the **open-below** corridor (3 probes) and down pick (up to 3 hits). At most 5·2 + 4 + 19·4 + 12·6 = 162 picks per scan. Hit pointers are only compared.
3. **Walk** the selected cells (`ForEachReference`, at most 8192 visits), turning hit pointers into live references; in mode 1 the walk stops as soon as every hit pointer is found. Mode 2 also looks at every `TREE` reference whose bound sphere reaches within 128 units of the camera-to-feet segment (`nearFan`).
4. **Kinds** (`extKindOf`): plain `REFR` with base Static or MovableStatic = structure; Tree = tree; everything else (actors, doors, containers, activators, furniture, flora, items, lights, markers of other types) is never touched. Terrain is not a reference (`rayNoRef`).
5. **Classify** (`classifyExterior`), in this order:
   - a structure that no ray hit is not a candidate (silent);
   - no usable OBND → skip `no-bounds`;
   - neither a ray hit nor (trees) cover or canopy → silent;
   - the floor probe **under the feet** hit it → skip `floor-contact` (the dock, bridge deck, rock or step the player stands on; wave 2c: the four outer probes no longer refuse a claim, see n2);
   - **large** → skip `large`: a structure whose widest local span ≥ `fExteriorMaxSpan` (1024), or ≥ 1536 tall, or ≥ 640 in both spans and ≥ 512 tall (a building shell); a tree ≥ 1536 wide;
   - a cover **or overhang** (below) that **stands on the player's ground** (box bottom within 16 of the feet or lower, both horizontal spans at least `fExteriorCoverFootprint`, 256) → skip `ground`: a shell or wall piece next to the player (structures only);
   - a cover or overhang whose base model path starts with `Dungeons\` or `Landscape\` (after an optional `meshes\` and `DLC0n\`) and whose box bottom is within 16 of the feet or lower → skip `ground-kit`: halls, facades, cave walls and rocks at their own doors (wave 2c, re-review M1 fix 2); a lifted dungeon bridge or a rock over a cave mouth still opens;
   - **cover** → hide (`ext-cover`): the **upward pick hit this reference** (collision fact) and the vertical column above the feet, from 160 units (above the head and above clutter such as a hay bale, 148 at most beside the player) up to the camera height, crosses its oriented box (no margin on entry; 48 units while hidden, the pick still required);
   - **overhang** → hide (`ext-overhang`): a ray hit it at least 192 units above the feet within 384 units of them in the ground plane, and the space under that hit is **open**: three horizontal probes at 144 units over the feet, from the player to 32 units past the ground point under the hit (straight and 32 units to either side), are **all** clear of any collision, and a pick down from `fExteriorDownStart` (16) below the hit to 160 over the feet does not meet the same reference;
   - **canopy** (mode 2, trees) → hide (`ext-canopy`): a camera-to-target segment crosses the tree's box at or above 160 units over the feet (`pierces`);
   - otherwise skip `beside`: a wall, a rock face, the roof of a building the player stands behind (its walls close the corridor), a thin wall on the sightline (it crosses the corridor), a tree trunk on a ray.
6. **Dwell, caps, hide, restore**: wave 1's table and machinery. 0.2 s to enter, 0.6 s to leave; at most `iExteriorMaxHidden` (16, max 32) tracked at once, nearest kept (a strictly nearer candidate evicts the farthest, cause `evicted`); at most `iHideMaxPerFrame` new hides per scan, nearest first; a root already app-culled is never taken; `SetAppCulled(true)` on the 3D root; a hidden piece that stops qualifying and is met by the probe under the feet, or by an outer probe within 24 of the feet's height, is restored in that scan (`underfoot`); one whose handle died or whose root changed is restored (`unload`). Exit thresholds are looser while hidden (cover box +48, overhang clear −24 and reach +64). A scan that could not read the grid, the world or the floor hides nothing new. Moving from one exterior cell into the next keeps exterior hides (`keepAcrossCells`); every other cell change restores at once, as in wave 1.

### The bound

At any moment the number of hidden exterior references is at most `iExteriorMaxHidden` (default 16, hard limit 32) and at most the number of tracked slots. Each one is either (a) a structure hit this scan by a ray from the camera to a point within 128 units of the player, which does not stand on the player's ground with both spans at least the cover footprint and is not a dungeon or landscape kit piece at the player's ground level, and which either the upward pick over the player's head also hit, or whose hit lies 192+ units above the feet within 384 units with a collision-free corridor under it and no part of itself below the hit (from `fExteriorDownStart` down) to head height, or (b) in mode 2, a tree the upward pick hit, or whose box is crossed above the head by one of those 19 segments. Nothing is selected by height or radius alone, nothing large, nothing the player stands on, no terrain. A hidden reference that stops qualifying is restored after 0.6 s.

Per-route expectations below use this bound plus the data predictions.

### Roofs of buildings the player is not under

In an exterior the player is never inside a building (interiors are other cells). Whole building shells are one reference: the large ones fail the size test; the smaller ones (`WRHouseShack01` 734 × 477 × 503 and many more, review M1) are `ground` whether the upward pick hit them or the overhang test passed (wave 2c: in `e71a03d` only cover was guarded, and a doorway let the overhang branch through, re-review M1), and otherwise only `beside`. Dungeon and landscape kit at the player's ground level (halls, facades, cave walls, rocks) is `ground-kit` whatever its footprint. Where a building is assembled from pieces (Markarth), a roof piece hit above the head is hidden only if the corridor under it is free, which the building's own walls prevent. `MrkMillRoof01` (roof only, z 136..422) behind its walls is the portable test case. **Hypothesis:** in game the walls' collision blocks the 144-unit probes as it blocks the box model; buildings with open ground floors (arcades, porches) will open up, which is the intended look.

### Trees: decision

Trees are **supported, opt-in (mode 2)**, and **off in mode 1**. Reason: app-culling a tree root is honoured by the same cull test as any static (binary, above); distant LOD is a separate object. Tree cover needs the upward pick to hit the tree (collision fact, review m6). Canopies are very likely without collision (**Hypothesis**); then tree cover never happens and only `canopy` takes trees, by box geometry, a weaker bound: a pine's box is about 950 wide and 2500 tall, so a tree whose trunk is up to ~475 units from a sightline counts. Mode 2 is therefore a separate look for the owner to judge, not the default.

## What could go wrong in the first game run

1. **Not compiled on Windows.** New engine surface: `RE::TES::GetSingleton`, `TES::gridCells/currentGridX/currentGridY/interiorCell`, `GridCellArray::GetCell/length/cells`, `TESObjectCELL::IsAttached`, `FormType::Tree`. All declared in the pin (`include/RE/T/TES.h:77,211-222`, `include/RE/G/GridCellArray.h:26-34`, `include/RE/T/TESObjectCELL.h:212,254`), reached through `RE/Skyrim.h`.
2. **The open-below picks are stricter now** (every corridor probe clear, and the down pick must not meet the same reference). A post, a hay bale above 144, a crossbeam of the neighbouring stable segment under its own roof (same reference), or a pick starting inside a thick roof that reports the slab's underside can close the overhang: the neighbouring segment then stays drawn and the player is partly covered again. Trace: `skip … beside` for 0627E2/E4, `downSame` and `open` against `probed`. Whether Havok reports a hit for a ray that starts inside a shape, and on which side, is not known here (NOT_RUN); a start 16 below the hit was chosen as "just below" per the review.
3. **Façade and doorway pieces.** `MrkSilverfishInnPart01` (448 × 385 × 663) at the Markarth inn exit is now `ground` if the upward pick hits it (385 ≥ 256). Doorway, hall and frame pieces thinner than 256 (the two door-exit lists above) can still be `ext-cover` if a lintel or frame is over the player; X12 and X15 show three.
4. **Box over-approximation**: a piece whose OBND is much larger than its mesh (an L-shaped piece, a tilted rock) can count as covering the player where its mesh is not overhead. Tilted references are handled by the full rotation, but the OBND box itself may be loose.
5. **Trees (mode 2) may hide more than the canopy actually blocks** (box ~950 wide for a pine); the cap (16) bounds it.
6. **Grid self-check at cell borders.** A one-frame mismatch between the player's parent cell and the position is only counted; if the parent cell lags for more than 1 s (10 scans) FCS-63 appears and the exterior rule pauses. A persistent reference's base cell is not walked: such hits are `unmatched` and never hidden.
7. **Cost.** Up to 162 picks per 0.1 s scan, and a walk of up to 4 cells; mode 2 dereferences every reference in those cells. Unmeasured (X8).
8. **Shadows and LOD.** A hidden roof's shadow may remain or vanish; a hidden tree's LOD billboard might reappear (Hypothesis above). Wave 1's open question about engine uses of the app-culled bit (AI line of sight, activation) applies outdoors too.
9. **Restore latency in menus** and **hook-silent grace** as wave 1 (items 7 and 8 there).
10. **Upward pick and collision-less canopies or decorations.** Pieces without collision (leaves, ropes, banners) are never cover now; a roof whose collision is simplified or offset may be missed by the single upward pick (then the stable segment over the player is only an overhang candidate, not cover).
11. **Cell-border keep (m4).** Outdoors a hidden piece now survives a cell change until the next scan's checks; if a cell detaches while the piece is still hit and kept, the `unload` check restores it at the next scan (0.1 s later). Interior↔exterior and lifecycle restores are unchanged.
12. **The probe instrument** reports collision, not rendering: hidden roofs still block its rays (wave 1 lesson 10). Screenshots and the rendered-visibility check decide.
13. **Floor protection is now the probe under the feet only** (re-review n2). A piece the player is about to step onto (an outer probe meets it) can be claimed until the player stands on it; if it is hidden by then, the `underfoot` restore brings it back in that scan (at most 0.1 s, plus the next frame). An Architecture piece that reaches the ground with a footprint under 256 and is not kit (a narrow shack, a room piece such as `RifRmSmExSm01`, a balcony such as `MrkKeepDoorwayBalcony01`) is kept drawn only if neither the upward pick nor the overhang test takes it; the box census below lists the ones that remain at door exits.
14. **The kit rule is a path rule.** Pieces under `Dungeons\` or `Landscape\` at the player's ground level never open, including any meant as a canopy over a dungeon exterior door (none was found in the census); mods that file architecture under those folders behave the same (out of scope, D55-7).
15. **Trace worker (n6).** With `iHideTrace=1` the trace worker keeps polling every 100 ms until the process exits when the 7200 exterior rows are not all used; no I/O, recorded rather than fixed.
